Meet Gentle Blossom 💙

Gentle Blossom started from Leilannie's love of making bracelets and cute things by hand. Her family is helping her learn how ideas become a real little business — one creation, one customer, and one lesson at a time.

Her parents help manage the account, messages, orders and safety. The goal is simple: have fun creating, learn, and never get discouraged.

“It’ll be difficult, but don’t get discouraged.”
